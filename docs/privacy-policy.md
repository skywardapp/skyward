# Skyward privacy policy

Skyward is a local-only app.

## What Skyward does not collect

Skyward does not require an account and does not collect, store, sell, or
share personal data on a Skyward-operated server. The project has no backend,
no analytics, and no advertising SDKs.

## Data that stays on your device

Skyward stores your settings, saved locations, rules, and cached event data on
your device so the app can work offline and schedule reminders.

If you grant location access, Skyward uses your device's coarse location only
to help you choose or update a saved location. That location does not leave
your device except as part of direct requests to the public data providers
listed below when the app refreshes nearby terrestrial events.

## Network requests Skyward makes

Skyward connects directly from your device to:

- NOAA SWPC, for aurora forecast data
- NASA EONET, for terrestrial event data
- NASA/JPL, for comet orbital data

Those providers receive the network metadata that any HTTPS request normally
includes, such as your IP address and user agent.

## Notifications and permissions

Skyward may request notification permission so it can show reminders you have
configured. On Android, the exact-alarm permission is requested only on the
builds that need it for precise reminders.
